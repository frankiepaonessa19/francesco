## Concept 1 — "The Super Blind Spot"

**1. Hook type:** Warning / loss aversion

**2. Copy**
- **Headline (under 40 chars):** Is your super quietly underperforming?
- **Primary text (under 125 chars):** Most Australians never check the fees eating their super. A 30-minute review could change your retirement. Book free.

**3. Visual concept**
A man in his early 50s sits at his kitchen table at dusk, reading a superannuation statement on paper, reading glasses pushed up, hand on forehead — not despairing, just *concentrating*. Warm domestic light, laptop open beside him. The emotion is "moment of realisation", not fear. No charts, no red arrows — the human moment is the hook.

**4. Higgsfield prompts**

*1:1 version:*
> Photorealistic candid portrait of an Australian man in his early 50s sitting at a warm timber kitchen table in the early evening, reading a printed financial statement, reading glasses resting low on his nose, one hand touching his forehead in quiet concentration, open laptop glowing softly beside him, modern Australian home interior with eucalyptus branch in a vase blurred in background, warm tungsten practical lighting mixed with cool dusk window light, shot on 85mm lens at f/1.8, shallow depth of field, natural skin texture, editorial documentary photography style, natural light, subtle film grain, aspect ratio 1:1

*4:5 version:*
> Photorealistic candid vertical portrait of an Australian man in his early 50s at a warm timber kitchen table in the early evening, reading a printed superannuation statement, reading glasses low on his nose, hand on forehead in quiet concentration, laptop glowing beside him, modern Australian home with soft dusk light through window, warm tungsten practicals, shot on 85mm lens at f/1.8, shallow depth of field, natural skin texture, editorial documentary photography, natural light, subtle film grain, aspect ratio 4:5

**5. Text overlay:** Single line, lower third: **"When did you last check your super fees?"** (small, clean sans-serif — keep under 15% of frame)

**6. Compliance flag:** ⚠️ NEEDS SIGN-OFF.
- "Quietly underperforming" implies a factual claim about super funds — must be defensible or softened to a question (currently phrased as a question, keep it that way).
- "Could change your retirement" edges toward an outcome claim — acceptable as conditional, but compliance should confirm.
- Australian financial services rules (ASIC RG 234): ad must carry general advice warning and AFSL details on landing page at minimum. Confirm whether the ad itself needs the disclaimer.

## Concept 2 — "Two Futures"

**1. Hook type:** Curiosity / contrast

**2. Copy**
- **Headline (under 40 chars):** Same salary. Very different retirements.
- **Primary text (under 125 chars):** The difference isn't luck — it's a plan. See what a structured wealth strategy looks like. Free discovery call.

**3. Visual concept**
Split-frame diptych feel in a single image: a woman around 60 on a coastal walk at golden hour (Sydney/Great Ocean Road energy), relaxed, mid-laugh with a takeaway coffee. The image sells the *outcome state* — freedom, not money. Aspirational but attainably Australian: no yachts, no champagne. The contrast lives in the copy, not the image, keeping it compliant and warm.

**4. Higgsfield prompts**

*1:1 version:*
> Photorealistic lifestyle photograph of a fit Australian woman around 60 years old walking along a coastal clifftop path at golden hour, genuine relaxed laugh, holding a takeaway coffee cup, linen shirt and walking shoes, ocean and sandstone cliffs softly blurred behind her, warm golden backlight with natural lens flare, shot on 85mm lens at f/2, shallow depth of field, natural skin texture with realistic wrinkles, candid editorial travel photography style, natural light, aspect ratio 1:1

*4:5 version:*
> Photorealistic vertical lifestyle photograph of a fit Australian woman around 60 walking a coastal clifftop path at golden hour, genuine relaxed laugh, takeaway coffee in hand, linen shirt, ocean and sandstone cliffs blurred behind, warm golden rim light, natural flare, shot on 85mm lens at f/2, shallow depth of field, realistic skin texture, candid editorial photography, natural light, aspect ratio 4:5

**5. Text overlay:** None — let the image breathe. Contrast hook is carried entirely by headline/primary text. (Best-performing variant for Meta's text penalty.)

**6. Compliance flag:** ⚠️ NEEDS SIGN-OFF.
- "Very different retirements" + aspirational imagery could be read as an implied performance promise. Mitigation: copy attributes the difference to "a plan", not returns. Compliance to confirm wording.
- Do not add any numbers/percentages to overlay without substantiation.
- Confirm imagery doesn't need "not a real client" disclosure under Titan's internal policy.

## Concept 3 — "The Second Opinion"

**1. Hook type:** Social proof / authority

**2. Copy**
- **Headline (under 40 chars):** Australians are getting second opinions
- **Primary text (under 125 chars):** Already have an adviser? Smart investors still get a second opinion. Titan Wealth Australia — independent-minded advice.

**3. Visual concept**
Over-the-shoulder shot in a bright, modern Australian office: a female adviser (30s–40s, professional but approachable, sleeves rolled) walking a couple through a single clean document across a light oak table. Genuine mid-conversation moment — client nodding, adviser gesturing at the page. Signals expertise + trust + transparency. Harbour-city daylight through big windows.

**4. Higgsfield prompts**

*1:1 version:*
> Photorealistic corporate lifestyle photograph, over-the-shoulder view of a professional Australian female financial adviser in her late 30s with sleeves rolled up, explaining a single clean printed document to a couple in their late 50s across a light oak meeting table, genuine mid-conversation gestures, bright modern office with floor-to-ceiling windows and soft city view bokeh, abundant natural daylight, neutral warm colour palette, shot on 85mm lens at f/2.2, shallow depth of field, natural skin texture, authentic editorial corporate photography, no stock-photo stiffness, aspect ratio 1:1

*4:5 version:*
> Photorealistic vertical corporate lifestyle photograph, over-the-shoulder view of an Australian female financial adviser late 30s, sleeves rolled, walking a couple in their late 50s through one clean printed document at a light oak table, genuine mid-conversation moment, bright modern office, floor-to-ceiling windows with soft city bokeh, abundant natural daylight, warm neutral palette, shot on 85mm lens at f/2.2, shallow depth of field, natural skin texture, authentic editorial corporate photography, aspect ratio 4:5

**5. Text overlay:** Small badge-style line, top corner: **"Free second-opinion review"** (one line, minimal footprint)

**6. Compliance flag:** ⚠️ NEEDS SIGN-OFF.
- "Independent-minded" — the word "independent" is restricted under s923A of the Corporations Act in Australia; even "independent-minded" is high-risk. Legal MUST clear or replace (e.g. "client-first advice").
- "Smart investors still get a second opinion" implies people without one aren't smart — soft risk, but check brand tone.
- "Australians are getting second opinions" is a social-proof claim — needs substantiation or rewording to "Get a second opinion".
- AI-generated people presented as advisers/clients: confirm Titan policy on synthetic imagery in financial promotions and whether disclosure is required.

## Production notes
- All three prompts avoid text-in-image (AI text renders badly and Meta penalizes it) — overlays to be added in Figma/Canva after generation.
- Generate 4+ seeds per prompt; pick for natural hands and eyes first.
- Suggested test structure: run all 3 concepts at 1:1 and 4:5, $20/day each, kill anything under 1% CTR after 3 days.
- Landing page must carry AFSL number, general advice warning, and privacy policy before any of these go live.
