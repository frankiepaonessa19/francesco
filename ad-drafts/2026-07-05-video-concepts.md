## Concept 1 — "The Super Blind Spot" (video)
**Runtime:** ~22 seconds · **Format:** 4:5 feed + 9:16 Reels crop · **Hook type:** Warning / loss aversion

### 1. Hook (0–3s)
Extreme close-up of a printed super statement sliding into frame on a kitchen table, one line circled in pen. Caption punches in word-by-word: **"When did you last check your super fees?"** No music yet — just the paper sound, then a low pulse starts.

### 2. Shot list

**Shot 1 — The statement (0–3s, 3s generation)**
- **Camera direction:** Slow top-down push in
- **Runway prompt:** *Close-up top-down shot: a printed superannuation statement lying on a warm timber kitchen table, a hand slides it into frame and circles a line with a pen, slow push in toward the circled figure, warm evening tungsten light, shallow depth of field, photorealistic, subtle handheld drift*
- **Reference image:** New Higgsfield still — top-down macro of statement on the Concept 1 timber table (match set/lighting). Prompt: *"Photorealistic top-down close-up of a printed financial statement on warm timber table, pen resting beside it, warm tungsten evening light, 85mm macro, shallow DOF, 4:5"*

**Shot 2 — The realisation (3–9s, 6s generation)**
- **Camera direction:** Slow lateral dolly right, eye level
- **Runway prompt:** *Medium shot: a man in his early 50s at a kitchen table in the evening reading a financial statement, reading glasses low on his nose, he pauses and touches his forehead in quiet concentration, slow lateral dolly right, warm tungsten practicals mixed with cool dusk window light, photorealistic, cinematic, natural micro-expressions*
- **Reference image:** The existing Concept 1 hero still (man at kitchen table) — use the best seed from the image campaign directly.

**Shot 3 — The comparison (9–15s, 6s generation)**
- **Camera direction:** Static over-the-shoulder, screen glow
- **Runway prompt:** *Over-the-shoulder shot: the same man now leaning toward an open laptop on the kitchen table, screen glow on his face, he scrolls slowly, subtle nod as he reads, static camera with gentle breathing movement, warm room now darker so laptop light dominates, photorealistic, cinematic*
- **Reference image:** New Higgsfield still — same man/set, OTS angle on laptop. Prompt: *"Photorealistic over-the-shoulder shot of Australian man early 50s at night leaning toward laptop at timber kitchen table, screen glow on face, dark warm room, 85mm, shallow DOF, 4:5"* (reuse Shot 2 seed/character reference for consistency)

**Shot 4 — The exhale (15–19s, 4s generation)**
- **Camera direction:** Slow push in to medium close-up
- **Runway prompt:** *Medium close-up: the man closes the laptop halfway, leans back, small relieved exhale and faint smile, slow push in, warm lamp light, photorealistic, natural relaxed body language*
- **Reference image:** Crop/variant of Shot 3 still, laptop half-closed.

**Shot 5 — CTA card (19–22s, motion graphics — not Runway)**
- Titan Wealth Australia logo on off-white, single line + button. Built in edit, not generated.

### 3. Voiceover / caption script (synced)
- **0–3s (Shot 1):** *(no VO — paper sound, pulse begins)* Caption: "When did you last check your super fees?"
- **3–9s (Shot 2):** VO: "Most Australians set and forget their super. And fees quietly do their work in the background." Caption mirrors key phrase: "set and forget"
- **9–15s (Shot 3):** VO: "A thirty-minute review can show you exactly what you're paying — and what your options are." Caption: "30-minute review. No cost."
- **15–19s (Shot 4):** VO: "That's it. Now you know." Caption: "Clarity feels good."
- **19–22s (Shot 5):** VO: "Titan Wealth Australia. Book your free super review." 

### 4. CTA (final 3–5s)
Card: **"Book your free super review"** → button "Learn more". Logo + AFSL line in small print at bottom of card.

### 5. Compliance flag ⚠️ NEEDS SIGN-OFF
- "Fees quietly do their work" implies fund fees are harming viewers — factual framing must be defensible; consider softening to "fees add up".
- "Can show you exactly what you're paying" — confirm the review deliverable actually does this.
- "No cost" claim must be true with no conditions, or state them.
- General advice warning: confirm whether it must appear in-video (end card small print) or landing page only per Titan's AFSL compliance policy (ASIC RG 234).
- Synthetic actor: confirm policy on AI-generated people in financial promotions and any disclosure requirement.

## Concept 2 — "Two Futures" (video)
**Runtime:** ~26 seconds · **Format:** 4:5 feed + 9:16 Reels crop · **Hook type:** Curiosity / contrast

### 1. Hook (0–3s)
Hard cut between two worlds: 1 second of a grey office lift lobby, then 2 seconds of golden-hour clifftop coastline. Caption: **"Same salary. Very different retirements."** The whiplash contrast is the scroll-stopper.

### 2. Shot list

**Shot 1 — The grey world (0–1.5s, 2s generation, trimmed)**
- **Camera direction:** Static wide, flat light
- **Runway prompt:** *Static wide shot: a bland grey office lift lobby, fluorescent light, a few blurred office workers waiting, muted desaturated colour grade, slight CCTV feel, photorealistic*
- **Reference image:** New Higgsfield still. Prompt: *"Photorealistic static wide of grey corporate lift lobby, fluorescent flat lighting, desaturated, blurred office workers waiting, 35mm look, 4:5"*

**Shot 2 — The golden world (1.5–3.5s, 2s generation)**
- **Camera direction:** Tracking shot alongside subject
- **Runway prompt:** *Tracking shot: a fit woman around 60 walking along a coastal clifftop path at golden hour, genuine relaxed laugh, takeaway coffee in hand, camera tracks alongside her at walking pace, warm golden backlight, ocean bokeh, photorealistic, cinematic lens flare*
- **Reference image:** The existing Concept 2 hero still (clifftop woman) — best seed from image campaign.

**Shot 3 — The morning ritual (3.5–10s, 6s generation)**
- **Camera direction:** Slow push in from wide to medium
- **Runway prompt:** *Wide to medium slow push in: the same woman around 60 sitting on a coastal bench with coffee, looking at the ocean, wind gently moving her hair and linen shirt, unhurried and content, golden hour side light, photorealistic, calm cinematic pacing*
- **Reference image:** New Higgsfield still — same woman (character reference from Shot 2 seed) seated on coastal bench. Prompt: *"Photorealistic wide shot of fit Australian woman around 60 sitting on coastal bench with takeaway coffee, golden hour, ocean behind, linen shirt, 85mm, 4:5"*

**Shot 4 — The plan flashback (10–17s, 7s generation)**
- **Camera direction:** Slow orbit around table
- **Runway prompt:** *Slow orbital shot: a couple in their late 50s at a light oak table with a financial adviser, one clean document between them, adviser gesturing calmly, bright modern office with floor-to-ceiling windows, natural daylight, photorealistic, warm neutral grade, genuine conversational body language*
- **Reference image:** The existing Concept 3 adviser still (reuse from image campaign — it becomes the "how" beat in this story).

**Shot 5 — Back to the coast (17–22s, 5s generation)**
- **Camera direction:** Slow pull back / drone rise
- **Runway prompt:** *Aerial pull-back shot: the woman walking the clifftop path at golden hour seen from behind, camera slowly rises and pulls back revealing the coastline stretching ahead, warm expansive light, photorealistic, uplifting cinematic ending*
- **Reference image:** New Higgsfield still — behind-view wide of clifftop path. Prompt: *"Photorealistic aerial view from behind of woman walking coastal clifftop path at golden hour, long coastline ahead, expansive warm light, 4:5"*

**Shot 6 — CTA card (22–26s, motion graphics — not Runway)**
- Logo card, built in edit.

### 3. Voiceover / caption script (synced)
- **0–3.5s (Shots 1–2):** *(sound design: fluorescent hum → cut to ocean and gulls)* Caption only: "Same salary. Very different retirements."
- **3.5–10s (Shot 3):** VO: "The difference between these two futures usually isn't income. It's whether anyone ever sat down and made a plan." Caption: "It's not luck. It's a plan."
- **10–17s (Shot 4):** VO: "Titan Wealth Australia builds that plan with you — your super, your investments, your timeline." Caption: "Super · Investments · Retirement"
- **17–22s (Shot 5):** VO: "So the future you're walking toward is the one you actually chose." Caption: "Choose your future."
- **22–26s (Shot 6):** VO: "Book a free discovery call with Titan Wealth Australia."

### 4. CTA (final 3–5s)
Card: **"Book a free discovery call"** → button "Sign up". Logo + AFSL line small print.

### 5. Compliance flag ⚠️ NEEDS SIGN-OFF
- The grey-vs-golden contrast implies advice causes a better retirement outcome — this is the core creative risk. Mitigation is that VO attributes the difference to *having a plan*, never to returns. Legal must approve the framing.
- "Very different retirements" — same conditional-claim review as the still campaign.
- No performance numbers, projections, or percentages anywhere in VO or captions — keep it that way through edits.
- Reuses the Concept 3 adviser imagery: the s923A "independent" issue from the still campaign does NOT carry over (no such wording here), but confirm final VO script against restricted-terms list.
- Synthetic actors + voiceover: confirm disclosure policy for AI-generated people and AI voice in financial ads.

## Production notes
- **Character consistency:** Lock one seed per character in Higgsfield and use it as a character/style reference for every still feeding Runway — Gen-4.5 holds identity well when the input frames match.
- **Generate long, trim short:** Generate each shot 1–2s longer than the edit needs; Gen-4.5's first and last ~0.5s are the most stable for cut points.
- **Aspect ratio:** Generate 4:5 natively; frame action centre-safe so the 9:16 Reels crop works without regenerating.
- **Sound:** VO with an Australian accent (ElevenLabs or licensed VO artist — see compliance note on AI voice). Music: low pulse for Concept 1, warm acoustic swell for Concept 2.
- **Test structure:** Run both against the still campaign, $30/day each; video usually wins Reels placement, stills win feed — judge on cost per booked call, not CTR.
- **Before publishing:** AFSL number + general advice warning on end card and landing page; legal sign-off on both scripts as written.
